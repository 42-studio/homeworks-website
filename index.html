02 / THE RESTORATION PROCESS

Restored with the RepairCare DryFlex system.

A permanent, flexible join between new and original timber — as specified by English Heritage and the National Trust for period timber repairs.

STEP 01

Strip back

The box sash is stripped back to bare wood and the rotten timber removed. The surface is then treated with Dry Fix WS — a wood stabiliser.

STEP 02

Splice & bond

Timber splices are made and bonded with the RepairCare DryFlex system — a permanent, flexible join that flexes with the timber, so joints never break open and water can't get in.

STEP 03

Insert splices

The prepared timber splices are inserted into the cleaned-back sections of the frame.

STEP 04

New end boxes

New end boxes are made and installed to rebuild the sash where needed.

STEP 05

Work & paint

Once cured, the resin can be planed, sawn, routed, nailed and screwed like wood, and painted with all solvent- and water-based paints.

STEP 06

Finish

The finished repair, ready and awaiting its final undercoat and top coat.
